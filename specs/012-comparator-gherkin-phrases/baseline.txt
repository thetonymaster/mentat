### Note on the 80% floor (affects T063)

`cmd/mentat` (69.4%) and `cmd/mentatctl` (31.4%) are **already below** the constitution's 80%
per-package floor at `0f9dcea`. 012 touches `cmd/mentat`.

T063's obligation is therefore read as: packages at or above 80% must stay at or above 80%;
packages already below must **not regress**. This is recorded rather than silently assumed,
because reinterpreting a gate without saying so is how gates quietly stop meaning anything.

The packages 012 actually changes and their floors to hold:

| Package | Baseline | Obligation |
|---|---|---|
| `internal/core` | 100.0% | stay ≥ 80% (expect ~100%) |
| `internal/steps` | 88.9% | stay ≥ 80% |
| `internal/engine` | 95.2% | stay ≥ 80% |
| `github.com/thetonymaster/mentat` (facade) | 99.1% | stay ≥ 80% |
| `cmd/mentat` | 69.4% | no regression below 69.4% |
